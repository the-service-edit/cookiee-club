Our story

Two sisters. Mum’s recipe.

The Cookiee Club was started by sisters Ruby and Rachel. Rachel lives with severe food allergies, so from day one the rule has been simple: make cookies more people can enjoy, without anyone feeling left out.

That’s why our kitchen is nut and egg free, and why our gluten-free cookies are baked in their own space. Every order is baked for you, then packed by hand and posted Australia wide.

Nut free, egg freeNo nuts in our house, ever, and none of our recipes use eggs. Some chocolates carry a “may contain”.
Gluten free, kept separateGF cookies are baked in their own space, approved by our allergen specialist.
Registered to bake all weekOur health inspector checks in every year, sometimes unannounced.

Good questions

How long until my cookies arrive?

Every order is baked to order. We bake and post within 2 to 4 business days, then express post usually takes 1 to 3 business days depending on where you are. Tracking is emailed when your parcel is posted.

Are your cookies nut and egg free?

Yes. We don’t handle nuts or eggs in our kitchen, and none of our recipes use eggs. Some chocolates we use (Cadbury and Nestlé) carry a “may contain” statement from the manufacturer. Read the full allergen guide.

How much is shipping?

Express post is a flat $16.20 anywhere in Australia, whatever the size of your order.

Can I choose my own flavours?

Yes. Build a box of 6 or 12 from any flavours on the menu. Build a box.

Does express shipping mean you bake it faster?

No. Every order joins the same baking queue. Express applies once your cookies are baked, packed and with Australia Post.
