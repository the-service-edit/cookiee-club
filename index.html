A NOTE FROM RUBY · DRAFT, RUBY TO REWRITE

Sorry we kept you waiting.

You found us faster than our little kitchen could keep up. Some of you waited weeks, and we didn't keep you in the loop the way we should have. That's on us.

So ordering works differently now. We only take what we can bake that week, you see your bake day before you pay, and we email you when your cookies go in the oven and when they're posted.

Ruby x

How ordering works now

01We cap each week's bake.When the week is full, the shop says so. Your order never sits in a queue for weeks.
02You see your bake day.Bake and dispatch dates show before checkout. Baked on the day, never frozen dough.
03We email every step.Confirmed, in the oven, posted with tracking. Express is usually 1 to 3 business days from there.
