Help

Questions, answered.

Can’t find it here? Message us.

How long until my cookies arrive?

Every order is baked to order. We bake and post within 2 to 4 business days, then express post usually takes 1 to 3 business days depending on where you are. Tracking is emailed when your parcel is posted.

Are your cookies nut and egg free?

Yes. We don’t handle nuts or eggs in our kitchen, and none of our recipes use eggs. Some chocolates we use (Cadbury and Nestlé) carry a “may contain” statement from the manufacturer. Read the full allergen guide.

How much is shipping?

Express post is a flat $16.20 anywhere in Australia, whatever the size of your order. That’s why the box of 12 is the best value per cookie.

Can I choose my own flavours?

Yes. Build a box of 6 or 12 from any flavours on the menu. Build a box.

Does express shipping mean you bake it faster?

No. Every order joins the same baking queue. Express applies once your cookies are baked, packed and with Australia Post.

How long do the cookies keep?

Best within [X] days in an airtight container. [Ruby’s storage and warming tip]

Can I change my order or address?

We can’t change an order once it’s placed. If you’ve made a mistake, like a typo in your address, message us straight away and we’ll do our best to fix it before it’s posted.

What if my cookies arrive damaged?

Send us your order number and a photo within 48 hours and we’ll replace or refund the damaged cookies. Refunds and damaged orders.

Do you do corporate gifts or events?

Sometimes, depending on our baking calendar. Send an enquiry with your date and numbers and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.
