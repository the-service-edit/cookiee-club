Allergens

Made in our nut and egg free kitchen: no nuts in the house, no eggs in our recipes. Our gluten-free cookies are baked in their own space, approved by our allergen specialist.

Express post, $16.20 flat anywhere in Australia, whatever you order. Tracking is emailed when your parcel is posted. In hot weather, choose an address where someone can bring it inside.
