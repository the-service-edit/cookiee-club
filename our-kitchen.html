Our kitchen

Made so more people can have one.

The Cookiee Club was started by sisters Ruby and Rachel, from their mum’s recipe. Rachel lives with severe food allergies, so from day one the rule has been simple: nobody gets left out of the cookie box.

Two sisters, Mum’s recipe, a bigger team.

We went from a few orders a day to hundreds overnight. We’ve since grown the team, so more hands bake and more hands pack, and we’re back to posting within 2 to 4 business days.

Ruby & Rachel x
