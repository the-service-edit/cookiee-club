Delivery & shipping.

Draft for Ruby and Rachel to review.
WhereAnywhere in Australia. We don’t ship overseas.
Cost$16.20 express post, flat, whatever you order.
BakingEvery order is baked to order and posted within 2 to 4 business days.
Express postUsually 1 to 3 business days after posting, depending on your location.
TrackingEmailed when your parcel is posted. “Label created” means it hasn’t reached Australia Post yet.

Does express mean you bake it faster?

No. Every order joins the same baking queue. Express applies once your cookies are with Australia Post.

Need it by a date?

Message us before you order and we’ll tell you honestly if we can make it.

Hot weather

Choose an address where someone can bring the parcel inside. Missed deliveries go to your local post office; collect them as soon as you can.

Wrong address?

Message us straight away. We can usually fix a typo before your order is posted, but not after.
