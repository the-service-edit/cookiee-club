Concept notes · The Service Edit

What’s new in v2.

Same look as v1. Underneath, it’s now a full store you can click through: every product, a working box builder, a cart and every help page.

Changed from v1

  • Removed the made-up “boxes left” count and the example bake dates. Delivery is stated as a rule: baked and posted within 2 to 4 business days, then express post.
  • Added the Build Your Own (12) and The Little Cookiee Club, both live on Ruby’s store since 11 October. Imperfect Gluten Free is now an option on the Imperfect Box.
  • One layout for every screen. v1 had a separate phone version that dropped the kitchen facts and the calendar; both now show on phones.
  • A real box builder: photo cards, + and − per flavour, “4 of 6” progress, Surprise me, and “Edit flavours” from the cart.
  • The cart offers one upgrade: a 6-box to a 12-box, keeping the flavours already picked.
  • Sold-out products swap the buy button for a restock alert form (the Imperfect Box shows this in the demo).
  • Product pages: real photos only, what’s in the box, allergens, delivery and refunds, and a sticky add-to-cart bar on phones.
  • New pages: Our kitchen and allergen guide, Drops, Delivery, Refunds, FAQ, Contact, Corporate, Cart.
  • Removed the unverified “Most ordered” label. Badges now state facts only.
  • The AI hero image is kept as it was.
  • Flavour images in “Pick your flavours” and the box builder are studio shots in v1’s menu style, made with Higgsfield (Nano Banana Pro) from Ruby’s real photo of each cookie. Product page galleries still use her untouched photos; the “What’s in the box” thumbnails use the studio shots.

Still needed from Ruby

  • Restock day and time, cookie size, shelf life and her warming tip.
  • Allergens per flavour (milk, soy, wheat) and the council registration number.
  • Confirm the flavour shots for Cinnamon Scroll, Biscoff White Choco, Oreo Milkshake and Plain Vanilla. They were made from her photos, but I matched which cookie is which by eye.
  • Confirm which Halloween photo is which flavour (matched from her product photos).
  • Contact email, ABN, Perth pop-up venue, reply time, and whether shipping is flat $16.20 or weight-based.
  • Sign-off on the delivery and refund drafts, and whether Afterpay is switched on.

Demo only

  • Stock states are set by hand. Forms show a thank-you but send nothing. Checkout stops at a placeholder page. In Shopify all of this comes from the platform.
