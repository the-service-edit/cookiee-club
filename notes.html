About this concept.

A full working preview of a new Cookiee Club website, built in your look with your real products, prices, flavours and photos. Click through anything: the menu, the box builder, the cart and every help page.

What it does

  • A visual box builder for 6 or 12: photo of every flavour, tap + to add, see your box fill up, “Surprise me”, and edit flavours from the cart.
  • Every product page answers the big questions on the page: what’s in the box, allergens, delivery and refunds.
  • Sold out? The buy button becomes a restock alert, so nobody who arrives from a TikTok leaves empty-handed.
  • A cookie calendar that updates itself from today’s date: what’s on now, what’s next, and when the shop is closed.
  • A sold out page with a live countdown to the next restock, and a closed page for breaks, each with an email sign up.
  • Customer reviews from their own TikToks, each linked to the original video.
  • Delivery, refunds, FAQ, contact and corporate pages, written in plain words.

About the photos

Every photo here started as one of your own. Each was reshot digitally from your photo of the real cookies: same cookies, toppings, bags and boxes, cleaned up and relit. The single-cookie shots were made the same way, one flavour at a time. Before anything goes live we check every image against the real thing together.

What’s not live yet

This is a preview: nothing here takes real orders, forms don’t send, and stock is set by hand. On Shopify, the cart, stock, checkout and Afterpay all run from your store, and the restock can open automatically at 7pm.
